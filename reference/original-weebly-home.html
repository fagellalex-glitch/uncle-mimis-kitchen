OUR STORY

Led by a professionally trained chef and baker, Uncle Mimi’s Kitchen makes everything fresh and local here on Martha’s Vineyard. Tammy started baking out of her kitchen in Chilmark and is happy to share her love of delicious breads and baked goods with all of you. Our small batch, artisanal products are made with the highest quality ingredients and we hope you enjoy every bite!   
​
We often are asked about the name and its origins…. As a toddler, Tammy’s nephew called her “Mimi” and lovingly assumed both she and her husband were uncles. His favorite place to eat, he insisted, was "Uncle Mimi’s Restaurant"—and it still is today.
